AUGUR-5 - AUGURY Scenes (TONAL LAB)

Ten sounds from AUSPICE, each with an AUGURY morph scene saved in the preset.
Load one, open the AUGURY tab and drag the bird: corner A is the sound as it is in AUSPICE;
B, C and D are variations of it, level-matched, so a flight changes the sound, not the volume.
RECALL plays a corner exactly. Loading a preset without a scene keeps the corners you have.

Needs an AUGUR-5 build that reads AUGURY scenes from presets (earlier versions load the sound
of corner A and ignore the scene).
