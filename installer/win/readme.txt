AUGUR-5 "3340" - analog modeling 5-voice polysynth - TONAL LAB

WHAT GETS INSTALLED (choose on the next pages)
  VST3        C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\AUGUR-5.vst3
              Ableton Live, Cubase, Nuendo, FL Studio, Reaper, Bitwig, Studio One, Cakewalk, Reason 12+
  Standalone  AUGUR-5.exe - plays without a DAW (choose your audio and MIDI devices in its settings)
  Presets     170 factory sounds (inside the plug-in) + the SUN ATLAS library (1000 presets)
              in %APPDATA%\TONAL LAB\AUGUR-5\Presets

AFTER INSTALLING
  Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins. AUGUR-5 appears as an instrument by TONAL LAB.
  Your own presets are kept when you uninstall or update.
  More preset packs (.zip) install from the plug-in: SETTINGS > INSTALL PACK (.ZIP).

NOT INCLUDED ON WINDOWS
  AAX (Pro Tools) needs Avid's SDK and PACE signing; it is not part of this release.
  AU exists only on macOS (see the macOS installer).
