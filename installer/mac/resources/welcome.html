AUGUR-5 “3340”

Analog modeling polysynth by TONAL LAB.

This installer adds:

  • VST3 to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 — Ableton Live, Cubase, Bitwig, FL Studio, Reaper, Studio One
  • Audio Unit to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components — Logic Pro, GarageBand
  • Standalone app to /Applications
  • SUN ATLAS (1000 presets) to your AUGUR-5 preset folder, next to the 170 factory sounds

Universal: Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later. After installing, rescan plug-ins in your DAW.

Español: instala el VST3, el AU (Logic), la app y la librería SUN ATLAS (1000 presets). Después vuelve a escanear los plug-ins en tu DAW.
